<DocSection id="intent" index="01" title="Why it exists" description="Evidence needs a role, not another decorative caption.">

Long portfolio pages contain screenshots, diagrams, system models, experiments, and content blocks. Figure Label gives each artifact a stable type and index so readers can understand what they are looking at before interpreting it.

**It should not become** a loud badge system or compete with the evidence it describes.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="usage" index="02" title="Usage" description="The smallest useful contract comes first.">

```astro
<FigureLabel
  type="SYS"
  index="03"
  title="State ownership"
  signal="Verified"
  as="div"
/>
```

Use one label per figure. Keep the title concrete and let the optional signal communicate verification or current state.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="variants" index="03" title="Variants">

- **Top** introduces the artifact before it appears.
- **Bottom** behaves as a conventional caption after the artifact.
- **Signal** adds one restrained status term without changing the taxonomy.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="accessibility" index="05" title="Accessibility">

The visible shorthand is combined into a readable `aria-label`. Decorative punctuation is hidden from assistive technology, and status text remains available as words rather than color alone.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="performance" index="06" title="Performance">

Server-rendered Astro markup with scoped CSS. It ships no client JavaScript and introduces no layout measurement or runtime state.

</DocSection>
