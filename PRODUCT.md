# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, frontend and product engineering leads, designers, founders, and technical peers evaluating Son Nguyen's product engineering work.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio explains how Son designs and ships AI products, interfaces, and software systems. Success means visitors can understand both the shipped outcomes and the reasoning, architecture, accessibility, and reusable systems beneath them.

## Positioning

The portfolio treats product work as connected evidence: case studies, production components, system diagrams, technical decisions, and writing reinforce one another instead of acting as isolated presentation pieces.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse selected work, inspect NoteX as the primary case study, read notes, understand the person behind the work, and evaluate reusable production components through a public component laboratory.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built with Astro components, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, CSS variables, semantic HTML, and native JavaScript.
- Client JavaScript is added only for focused interactions.
- Component documentation must render the same production components used by portfolio pages.
- Unknown personal, company, education, contact, and product evidence remains explicitly unverified instead of being invented.

## Brand Commitments

The visual language is Technical Wallpaper Editorial: off-white and deep-ink surfaces, Swiss grotesk typography, Geist Mono annotations, sparse IBM Plex Serif reflections, one-pixel rules, a restrained rust accent, and quiet purposeful motion. Atmospheric architecture and abstract imagery introduce professional substance; they do not substitute for product evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

- NoteX case-study content and architecture chapters in the repository.
- Six signature Astro components already used across Home, About, and NoteX.
- No verified portrait, company, education, contact, or product screenshot assets are currently available.
- Two original AI-generated editorial images are explicitly labeled as conceptual imagery, not a portrait, verified building, or NoteX screenshot. Provenance is recorded in `docs/EDITORIAL_ASSETS.md`.

## Product Principles

- Show production evidence rather than decorative claims.
- Explain why a system exists, not only what it looks like.
- Keep the interface lightweight, semantic, accessible, and responsive.
- Make reusable decisions visible without turning the portfolio into a generic component marketplace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic reading order, sufficient contrast, touch-friendly controls, and reduced-motion fallbacks are required across public routes.
